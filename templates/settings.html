Help with iPhone and Mac notifications

On iPhone, open this app from its Home Screen icon. If needed, open it in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen.

If permission is blocked, allow SecondBrain in Settings → Notifications on iPhone, or System Settings → Notifications on Mac. Focus modes can silence banners. HTTPS and a working server connection are required.

In Brave, open brave://settings/privacy, enable Use Google services for push messaging, and relaunch Brave. Brave cannot create Web Push subscriptions while that service is disabled.

The test reports whether the push service accepted the message. Check the notification on this device to confirm delivery.
